| `st-james-infirmary.json` | "St. James Infirmary" by "Joe Primrose" (Irving Mills), Gotham Music Service, © 1930. The first phrase of the refrain, from the scan on [IMSLP](<https://imslp.org/wiki/St._James%27_Infirmary_(Mills,_Irving)>). E minor, as printed. The original's cut time is written as 4/4 with the same rhythm, plus a one-beat pickup. The edition prints all three verses under one melody line. The rhythm follows the well-known third verse ("…Saw my baby there, / Stretched on a long white table, / So sweet, so cold, so bare"), which fits the printed notes one syllable per note, except in bar 2: there the E under "In-fir-ma-ry" is struck twice (eighth, then dotted half) so each syllable gets its own note. The syllable-by-syllable table is in `docs/overnight/st-james-verse3.md`. | Public domain in the US (published 1930; entered the public domain Jan 1, 2026). Not public domain in Canada. |
