# Words under the staff: decisions

Format: **date — decision.** Why. _Rejected:_ alternatives.

- **2026-10-07 — An optional `lyric` string on each note.** One syllable per note, 1 to 40 characters, checked by song.schema.json, `validateSong`, and the persisted-song guard. It travels with the note, so every edit that keeps a note (pitch, octave, duration, transpose, re-key, re-bar) keeps its syllable with no extra code, and deleting the note deletes it. _Rejected:_ a song-level `lyrics: { noteId: syllable }` map (every note edit would have to keep two structures in step, and an orphaned id is one more invariant to validate).
- **2026-10-07 — A split word is stored the way the source table prints it**, a trailing hyphen on every syllable but its last and a leading one on every syllable but its first. The ABC writer drops the leading hyphen and keeps the trailing one, which is what joins two syllables in a `w:` line; ABC-special characters are dropped and spaces become `~`, so a syllable can't shift the alignment or break the line.
- **2026-10-07 — `replaceTake` drops syllables.** A recorded take is new notes; the old notes' syllables go with them, the same way their chords do. Transpose keeps them.
- **2026-10-07 — The tutor snapshot carries no lyrics.** `toTutorSnapshot` builds each note field by field (beat, pitch, degree, beats), so the syllable never reaches the wire; a unit test pins that.
- **2026-10-07 — Words get their own `w:` line under the degree line.** The degree line stays next to the notes (it is the lesson); the words go below it. One slot per drawn glyph: the first glyph of a note takes its syllable and tied continuations take `*`, and abcjs skips rests on its own, so syllables stay aligned past rests and ties. Words show in every label mode, hidden included: they don't give the key away.
- **2026-10-07 — A "Words" switch beside "Drone on home", shown only when the song has words, on by default.** It lives in UI state (`showWords`), not the song. The staff header is being redesigned in another stream, so the switch is a self-contained component (`src/staff/WordsSwitch.svelte`) that the new toolbar can move as is.
- **2026-10-07 — St. James's syllables come from the "Syllable by syllable" table in docs/overnight/st-james-verse3.md by a one-off script**, matched to notes by bar.beat and pitch. All 28 rows matched 28 notes with no pitch mismatches and nothing left over. The script was not committed.
- **2026-10-07 — The words are regular weight; the degrees stay bold.** abcjs draws every `w:` line of a note as one `<text>` with one `<tspan>` per line and a single font (`vocalfont`), so `%%vocalfont` can't style the two lines apart. Instead, while words show, the staff writes the words line on every music line (so a syllable is always the second-to-last `tspan`, before abcjs's trailing empty one), and a scoped rule (`.notation.words .abcjs-lyric > tspan:nth-last-child(2)`) sets that line to 400. _Rejected:_ `%%vocalfont` alone (it would un-bold the degrees too).
- **2026-10-07 — Lyrics are measured in Jost 15 bold and drawn at 13.** abcjs leaves each note room for its lyric text's measured width. It measured in its default Times while CSS drew in Jost, so "Stretched" and "on" touched in bar 5. Measuring in the real face two sizes up (`vocalfont: "Jost 15 bold"`, with CSS pinning lyric text at 13px) leaves every syllable some air: the smallest gap between St. James syllables went from about 1 px to about 15 px at both 1280 and 1440, measured from SVG bounding boxes. The lyric lines sit a little lower under the staff as a result. _Rejected:_ `%%vocalspace` (abcjs uses it for vertical space only). A wider `staffwidth` was not tried.
