# Stream U1: layout (the music is the hero)

Format: **date — decision.** Why. _Rejected:_ alternatives.

- **2026-10-07 — The staff comes first in `<main>`, under a slim masthead; below it, two columns on screens 1100px and wider.** The left column holds the key question (Landing) and then `#chords`; the right column holds `#tutor` with the trust panel under it. Narrower screens stack these in one column, with the staff still first. This answers Matthew's and his friend's notes: "the music should be the hero", "the staff should never appear below the other elements", "much less scrolling". Every region id and accessible name stays (landing, staff, chords, tutor, piano, toolbar, key-prompt). The workspace grows to 80rem. **Deviation from the PRD's "empty state ... below it a blank staff":** the blank staff is now above the welcome too, since the brief puts the staff on top everywhere. _Rejected:_ a three-column layout (the tutor gets too narrow to read); keeping the welcome first when nothing is loaded (the staff would jump on the first load).
- **2026-10-07 — Measured above the fold, Ode to Joy loaded, demo awaiting a guess, today's 200px keyboard dock:**
  - **1280x800:** the masthead, the whole staff (both systems), the key question's heading, lead, "Hear the tune" and "It sounds", with the dock at the bottom. The "Home is" row starts at the dock's edge. Stream R's more compact keyboard brings it into view.
  - **1440x900:** all of the above, plus the whole "Home is" row. The guess buttons start at the dock's edge.
  - **390x844:** the masthead (three short rows), the staff header and staff, then the key question's heading, lead, and "Hear the tune" above the dock.
  - **No song loaded:** the masthead, a blank staff, the welcome with both song cards, and the tutor beside it at 1280 and 1440. At 390, the welcome and the first song card.

  Checked in both themes.

- **2026-10-07 — The staff drawing is capped at 56rem wide and its title drawn small (abcjs `titlefont`, 13pt).** abcjs scales its SVG to fill the container, so at 1280 the two systems grew to about 410px and pushed the key question under the dock. The cap sits on a wrapper around the abcjs host, because abcjs sizes the host with a percentage `padding-bottom`, which resolves against the host's parent; capping the host itself left about 90px of blank space under the music. The small title also prints small; the masthead names the song on screen. While no song is loaded, the "Untitled" title is hidden. _Rejected:_ dropping the title from the ABC (the lead sheet would lose it); computing `staffwidth` from the container (a redraw on every resize).
- **2026-10-07 — The song chooser is a small "Song: Ode to Joy | St. James Infirmary" control in the masthead once a song is loaded.** With nothing loaded, the welcome keeps its two song cards (`#song-chooser`, the welcome tip's anchor), with a shorter heading and tighter padding. Both looks are one component, `src/toolbar/SongPicker.svelte`, because Stream U1 owns `src/toolbar/` and only `Landing.svelte` in `src/finding/`. Loading goes through one line, `const openTune = loadDemo;`, which becomes `song.open` when Stream P adds per-song memory. Clicking the tune that's already open does nothing, so one click never wipes the user's edits. _Rejected:_ a `<select>` (some browsers fire `change` on arrow keys, which would load a song by accident); runtime feature detection of `song.open` (it would skip the demo setup that `loadDemo` does, and the contract for `open` isn't written yet).
- **2026-10-07 — Landing resets its key-question state when the song id changes,** not when its own button is clicked, since songs can now load from the masthead. Landing shows no card when it has nothing to say (a song is loaded, no prompt, no sound message), so the step column starts with the chords.
- **2026-10-07 — The staff header is one wrapping row:** Play/Stop, Undo/Redo, the chord-label segmented control (Roman, Nashville, Letters, Roman + letters, bound to `ui.labelStyle`), a "Scale degrees" switch (`ui.showDegrees`), a "Voice leading" switch (`ui.auditionVoicing`: off is "as-song", on is "from-candidate") with a (?) that opens the explainer from `content/explainers.json`, Print lead sheet, and "Change key or transpose". The new pieces are small components in `src/toolbar/` (History, LabelControls, VoiceLeading). The Cmd/Ctrl-Z shortcut moved with Undo/Redo into History. Switches are buttons with `role="switch"`, matching the masthead toggles. The label examples ("I IV V") moved to each segment's `title` to save width. The explainer opens on its own full-width line under the row (its wrapper is `display: contents`), so nothing floats over the music. Print CSS hides `#staff > .header` instead of the old `.toolbar` (`src/print.css` is the staff's print stylesheet). _Rejected:_ a popover for the explainer (it would cover the staff and need positioning code).
- **2026-10-07 — Transpose and re-key stay in Toolbar, collapsed in a `<details>` titled "Change key or transpose", as the last item of the staff header.** Closed, it is one pill in the row; open, it takes the full width under the row. `#toolbar` and its "Song tools" name stay on the section around the `<details>`, so the toolbar tip has a visible anchor. **Deviation:** the brief says "placed under the staff header". It sits in the header's row while closed, so the music doesn't lose a line, and opens under the row. _Rejected:_ its own line under the header (about 32px more above the music, every time).
- **2026-10-07 — ChordDropdown: the voice-leading toggle and explainer are gone (it still reads `ui.auditionVoicing`); in hidden mode it shows "Choose the key first..." and a "Go to the key question" button instead of candidates.** The button closes the dropdown and focuses `#key-prompt-title`, which scrolls it into view above the dock. The message takes the help paragraph's id, so the dialog's `aria-describedby` reads it. "No chord here" still shows if a chord is placed. Nothing else in the dropdown changed. Note: the comment on `AuditionVoicing` in `src/store/ui.js` still says the toggle lives in the dropdown; that file isn't this stream's.
- **2026-10-07 — The trust panel is collapsed in a `<details>` whose summary is its heading.** The region keeps its name ("How much should you trust the tutor?") and stays visible as one line under the tutor. Its content is unchanged.
- **2026-10-07 — Scroll padding for the dock.** App measures the dock with a ResizeObserver into `--dock-height` on `<html>`, and `global.css` sets `scroll-padding-bottom: var(--dock-height, 0px)` on `html`, the scrolling element. Focus and `scrollIntoView` then stop above the keyboard. The page keeps scrolling on the document, not an inner container, because the chord dropdown places itself in page coordinates. No wrapper in the new layout is positioned or transformed, so the dropdown's containing-block audit stays quiet. Piano.svelte is untouched.
- **2026-10-07 — Callout placement avoids controls, in pure, tested logic (`src/callouts/tour.js`).** `placeCallout` now takes `{ avoid }`, the boxes of the controls a tip must not cover. The component passes the key question's buttons and choice labels (the guess buttons are inside it), Play, and the masthead toggles. The old placement stays the first choice; if it covers a control, the tip tries the anchor's right side, then its left (only where it really fits), then above, below, over the anchor's own top, and the gaps between controls, keeping the first clear spot or else the one that covers least. Measured at 1280x800, 1440x900 and 390x844 through the whole tour after loading Ode to Joy: no tip covers any of those controls. Existing placement tests pass unchanged.
- **2026-10-07 — Next scrolls the next tip's anchor into view, like `block: "nearest"`, but further when needed.** At 390x844 the key question fills the screen, so with "nearest" the chord tip had nowhere to go but over "Make C major home". `scrollForTip` (pure, tested) works out the "nearest" scroll, predicts where the tip would land, and if that covers a control, scrolls the anchor to the top instead, within the page's scroll range. It uses `window.scrollBy`, smooth unless the viewer prefers reduced motion. **Deviation:** the brief says `block: 'nearest'`. This is "nearest" whenever that leaves the controls clear. _Rejected:_ always "nearest" (fails the phone requirement); always "start" (jumps the page when the anchor is already in view).
- **2026-10-07 — Callout copy revised for the new layout (draft for Matthew):** the staff tip comes second and points at Play and clicking notes. The key-prompt tip no longer repeats the prompt's own text. It had said "your answer is a guess, and you can change it any time", which the prompt's lead already says; it's now titled "Your first job: find home". The toolbar tip describes the collapsed "Change key or transpose" and the label buttons above the staff. The others are trimmed.
- **2026-10-07 — The tagline hides below 40rem,** where it cost a line. The masthead's toggles share the title's line where they fit, and the song picker wraps to the next line.
- **2026-10-07 — The key-selection request ("clicking the key is enough") is not built here.** That change lives in `src/finding/KeyPrompt.svelte`, which this stream doesn't own, and a parallel stream (`stream/o-key`) owns the key question. Flagged in the result so it isn't lost.
