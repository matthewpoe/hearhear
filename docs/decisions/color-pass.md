# Color pass and taller keys

Format: **date — decision.** Why. _Rejected:_ alternatives.

Matthew asked for "way more colorful" (the chords were the page's only color) and taller piano keys.

- **2026-10-07 — The primaries stay reserved for harmony. The interface gets two of Itten's Bauhaus secondaries.** Blue, yellow and red, each with its circle, triangle or square, mean tonic, subdominant and dominant (D3). Before a guess, a demo hides all function color, so any blue, yellow or red in the interface would read as a harmonic hint. The interface uses violet (`--accent`) and green (`--sound`) instead, and it never uses a function hue. Its decoration is bars and bands, not the function shapes. _Rejected:_ the primaries in "clearly non-semantic" blocks, because a blue bar beside a blue tonic chip can't be told apart from meaning; Itten's orange, which sits next to dominant red, especially in the dark theme (#f2837a); a bluer violet (#5b3cc4), which has the same luminance as tonic blue (1.07:1) and reads as blue to many color-blind viewers. The chosen #7a2a9e is a clear red-violet.
- **2026-10-07 — Color goes by role, not by region.** Violet is what you do: selected and pressed controls, primary actions, card bands, section headings, the wordmark and the focus ring. Green is what you hear: Play, the Listen buttons, the drone switch when on, and the keyboard dock's frame. The staff's own band is ink, like the notation. The masthead has a three-part bar across the top (violet, green, ink) and a solid violet wordmark block. _Rejected:_ one color per region (staff, step, tutor), which is decoration with no meaning and makes three colors compete with the function colors.
- **2026-10-07 — Contrast is AA in both themes, recorded in `tokens.css`.** Light: `--accent` 7.6 on paper and 7.9 on surface, and white on violet is 7.9. `--sound` is 5.1 on paper, and white on green is 5.3. Dark: `--accent` 8.4 and `--sound` 10.1 on paper, and their dark ink on each fill is 8.8 and 10.6. The soft tints keep ink at 10.5 or more and muted ink at 5.9 or more. The focus ring is `--accent`, so it is 7.6 (light) and 8.4 (dark) against the page. Inside a checked violet segment the ring switches to `--accent-ink` (7.9 and 8.8), so it doesn't disappear on its own fill. axe found 0 violations in 36 states. _Rejected:_ keeping the ink focus ring, which works but gives up the one control state every keyboard user sees.
- **2026-10-07 — The toolbar and the key box get their color from `global.css`, not their own files.** A parallel stream is restructuring `src/toolbar/*` and `KeyPrompt.svelte`. Pressed or checked controls under `#staff .header` and `#key-prompt` take the violet fill from `#id`-scoped rules in `global.css`, and so does the key question's heading. The drone switch is excluded so it stays green. If the restructure renames those regions, it should move these rules into the components as `var(--accent)` / `var(--accent-ink)`. _Rejected:_ editing those files, which would conflict with that stream.
- **2026-10-07 — Some components keep their ink buttons for now: the song picker, guided entry, guided path and callouts.** Other streams own those files. The masthead's pressed song and the landing's song cards are colored from `App.svelte` and `Landing.svelte` with `:global` selectors, so `SongPicker.svelte` itself is unchanged. "Show me how", the tour strip's primary button and the tip's Next stay ink until their owners switch `var(--ink)` to `var(--accent)`. _Rejected:_ editing those files from this branch.
- **2026-10-07 — Keys are `clamp(5.5rem, 16vh, 8.25rem)` tall, 1.5x the old 5.5rem from a 825px-tall window up.** They are 8rem at 1280×800 and capped at 8.25rem at 1440×900. On phones the cap is `clamp(5.5rem, 11vh, 7rem)`, about 5.8rem at 390×844, because the dock already takes a third of a phone screen. _Rejected:_ a fixed 8.25rem, which pushes the key question's home chips under the dock at 1280×800.
- **2026-10-07 — On screens 72rem wide or more, the dock's controls and help sit in an 18rem column beside the keys.** The taller keys then add no height: the dock is about 160px tall at 1280×800, down from about 170. The staff stays whole, and the home-note chips stay visible above the dock with the tour strip open. This was needed, not just tidier. With the controls stacked above taller keys, the beginner tips test (`tips.spec.js`) failed: "Same tune, any key" had no room between the staff and the dock and covered notes. Narrower than 72rem, the column would squeeze 29 white keys under their 1.75rem minimum, so the controls stack above the keys as before. _Rejected:_ shrinking the keys on short windows only (still failed at 40vh − 13rem); shortening the dock's help text (that's a copy change, and Matthew's call).
