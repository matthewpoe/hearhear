# Stream S: staff, chords, and tutor first-click friction

Format: **date — decision.** Why. _Rejected:_ alternatives. These are fixes for what two first-time-user walkthroughs (a musician and a non-musician) of the deployed app ran into. No contract, token, or dependency changed. Each fix was checked in a browser (Playwright, 1440x900 and 1280x800, light and dark themes, axe clean, no console errors) against a fixture-mode server.

## Staff

- **2026-10-07 — The whole note is the click target.** abcjs draws a note as separate paths (notehead, stem, accidental) inside one group, and the gaps between them take no clicks, so clicking the middle of a note usually missed (27 of 30 Ode notes in the walkthrough). After each draw, the staff puts a transparent rectangle behind each note group, covering its drawn shapes plus 3 SVG units of padding, so clicks anywhere on the note land on the group. All 30 of Ode's notes now open the dropdown from a click at their centre. The rectangle leaves out the group's text (the chord symbol and the degree under the note), which abcjs also puts in the group: a wide chord name such as "V7 · A7" would otherwise reach over the neighboring notes. Hover tints the rectangle (`--ink` at 10%) and outlines the notehead in `--ink-muted`; keyboard focus keeps its existing `--focus` outline and notehead stroke, and Enter and the arrow keys are unchanged. _Rejected:_ a CSS `stroke-width` hit area on the stem (still leaves the gap between stem and head); `pointer-events: bounding-box` (Chrome only).
- **2026-10-07 — Chord symbols on the staff follow the label style.** **Changes decision D3** ("the staff shows colored letter names like a lead sheet"). The brief for this stream asks for it, because with Roman selected the staff showed "D" and "A" while the chips said I and V. The staff writes the same text the chord chips show, from `chordView`: Roman "V7", Nashville "5⁷" (the chips' superscript seventh, written as a superscript character because ABC chord text is plain text), Letters "A7", Roman + letters "V7 · A7". Function colors, tentative italics, and the hidden-mode neutral mark are unchanged, and the redraw on a label-style change was already there (D17). The accessible name of each note says the same symbol. The print lead sheet prints this staff, so it follows the label style too (checked in print media). _Rejected:_ the brief's "I (D)" for Roman + letters: the chips directly below the staff show "I · D", and two spellings of one setting on one screen would read as two different things. Matthew may prefer letters on the printed lead sheet; that would be a print-only override, not built.
- **2026-10-07 — The chord symbol's ABC guard checks the first character, not for a root letter.** Numerals don't start with A to G, so the old guard (a chord name must start with a root letter) would have turned every numeral into "?". The text still drops quotes, newlines, `%`, and backslashes, and a symbol starting with one of ABC's placement marks (`^ _ < > @`), which would turn it into an annotation, reads as "?".
- **2026-10-07 — The empty landing has no staff.** App.svelte renders the staff only once the song has notes, so the landing shows no Play, no Print lead sheet, and no blank staff (the brief allows App.svelte for this one change). The staff's own "only with notes" checks around History, the label controls, voice leading, and the toolbar became dead and are removed, along with the style that hid the blank staff's "Untitled". A side effect: the beginner tour's staff tip ("Press Play and watch") now appears after a song loads, since a tip waits for its anchor; before, it pointed at a blank staff with a Play button that did nothing. _Rejected:_ hiding only the header buttons inside Staff.svelte (leaves an empty bordered box on the landing).

## Positions

- **2026-10-07 — One formatter names positions: "pickup, beat 4" and "bar 3, beat 2½".** `src/chords/where.js` (`whereOf`, `positionLabel`, `beatLabel`) replaces `chordView.whereOf`. Bar 0 reads "pickup". Halves, thirds, quarters, and sixths of a beat read as fraction characters (2½, 1⅓, 3¾); anything finer rounds to two decimal places. It is used everywhere the UI names a position: the dropdown's title, the chord chips, and the tutor's ideas on the chip row. Unit tests in `tests/chords/where.test.js`. _Rejected:_ "the and of 2" (longer, and wrong for thirds and quarters).

## Chord dropdown

- **2026-10-07 — A note click before the key was chosen already opened "Choose the key first"; the miss was the hit target.** With the hit area above, a pre-key click opens the dropdown in that state every time. The Chords panel's idle line now fits the state: before a song, "Load a tune, then click a note on the staff to try chords under it."; before the key, "Choose the key first, then click a note to try chords under it."; after, as before. The first of the three is beyond the brief's wording, but the old line pointed at a staff the landing no longer shows.
- **2026-10-07 — The dropdown refits on page scroll and resize, choosing its side again, and never scrolls the page.** The extended list's scroll area ended up under the sticky keyboard dock when the page scrolled under the open dropdown (a wheel at the end of its list scrolls the page, or the browser scrolls a hidden button into view). The placement already knew the dock; it only ran on open, on resize, and on content growth. Now `overscroll-behavior: contain` keeps a wheel inside the list from scrolling the page, and a page scroll or resize refits against the current view, choosing the side again so a dropdown whose anchor scrolled away doesn't shrink to a sliver. Growth of the content (the extended list, tutor ideas) still keeps the side, so the list scrolls inside rather than flipping. _Rejected:_ closing the dropdown on page scroll (loses the user's place in a long list).
- **2026-10-07 — A fade with "More below" marks a scrolled-off list.** macOS hides scrollbars until scrolling, so the cue can't be the scrollbar alone. A sticky gradient over the bottom edge of the dropdown, with "More below" in `--ink-muted`, shows while content is hidden below and disappears at the end; it takes no space and no clicks, and is `aria-hidden` (the list itself is in the accessibility tree). The scrollbar is also colored `--ink-muted` where the platform draws it.
- **2026-10-07 — "No chord here" is now "Remove this chord".** After a chord-row key placed a chord, the dropdown (correctly) stayed open with that chord marked Current and the remove action at the bottom; the action's wording read as a status line that contradicted the chord just placed. The behavior is unchanged.
- **2026-10-07 — Opening the dropdown starts audio if it isn't running.** Opening is a user gesture (a click, Enter on a note, or a chip), and the hover auditions that follow are not, so if no earlier gesture started audio (the landing's first-click unlock timed out, or the user only used the keyboard), the first hover was silent and logged "Audio is suspended". The dropdown now calls `unlock()` on open, before any audition, but only while Tone's audio context isn't running: `unlock()` also plays the sound-check chord, which would otherwise ring out (in C, over a tune in D) on the first note click of every visit. It reads the context state with a dynamic `import("tone")`, the same module instance the audio engine uses. A failure shows in the dropdown's existing "piano sounds didn't load, Try again" line. **Deviation from the contracts' layering:** only Stream B touches Tone elsewhere. A silent `resume()` export from `src/audio/index.js` would remove both the Tone import and the condition; requested as a contract change. _Rejected:_ calling `unlock()` on every open (the sound check on every note click); `drone(x)` then `drone(null)` as a silent wake (works, but would cut off a key-finder drone and reads as a trick).

## Tutor panel

- **2026-10-07 — The panel says when replies are recorded.** In fixture mode every question got the same recorded reply about Ode's bar 4, with nothing on screen saying so. The panel reads `/api/health` once on mount and, when `tutor_mode` is `fixture` (or a reply's `served_by` is `fixture`), shows "Demo mode: the tutor plays back recorded sample replies about Ode to Joy, so it may not answer your exact question." A failed health check leaves the notice off and logs a warning: it's informational, and the tutor's own requests keep their loading, failed, and retry states. `served_by` is added to the client's `SuggestionsEvent` typedef (it was already in the SSE contract).
- **2026-10-07 — Ask needs a tune and a question.** Ask is disabled while the question is empty and before any song is loaded, and Enter does nothing then; before a song the panel says "Load a tune first." and the text box has no placeholder ("Why does bar 4 feel unfinished?" appears once a song is loaded). "Show me options" and "Tell me" are unchanged: they build on the last reply and still send without a new question. **Changes the e2e golden path:** step 7 clicks Ask with an empty question; it needs a `fill` first (listed as a known gap, since this stream doesn't edit tests/e2e).

## Keyboard dock

- **2026-10-07 — The chord-row help line says "the A key".** "Bottom row keys play chords: the A key is the home chord (1), F is 4, G is 5. With a note's chord picker open, a letter key places that chord." "A is the home chord" read as the note A. The second sentence states the existing behavior (a chord-row key assigns to the open note once the key is confirmed). At 1280px the line wraps under the dock's controls, making the dock one line taller.
