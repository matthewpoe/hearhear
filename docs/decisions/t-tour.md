# Stream T: the beginner tour shows each tip with its subject

Format: **date — decision.** Why. _Rejected:_ alternatives.

Builds on Stream K's callouts (`docs/decisions/k-callouts.md`). Tips stay on by default.

- **2026-10-07 — Each callout carries a `when`: the app facts that must hold for it to show.** The facts are `songLoaded`, `keyChosen`, `playing`, `finderOpen`, `dropdownOpen`, and `chordPlaced` (`TourFacts` in `src/callouts/tour.js`), read from the song and UI stores and, for the ear finder, from the page. `isDue` and `nextCallout` are pure and tested. A tip shows when it isn't seen yet, its moment has come, and its subject is on screen. Anchor presence alone let "Hover a chord" and "Blue circle means home" point at the Load-a-song card, and "Same tune, any key" point at a locked control. _Rejected:_ a free-form condition string (needs a parser for six booleans); gating in the component per id (the order and conditions belong with the copy, in data).
- **2026-10-07 — A `doneWhen` fact counts a tip as seen once the user does what it asks.** Loading a song finishes the welcome, pressing Play finishes the staff tip, choosing a key finishes "find home", opening the ear finder finishes its tip, and opening the chord dropdown finishes "Hover a chord". It's level-based (the fact holds now), so a tip whose action already happened before it showed, such as a returning song with its key chosen, never shows. Seen tips persist as before. _Rejected:_ watching for transitions (misses actions taken while tips were off or before the tip mounted).
- **2026-10-07 — The welcome has no Next (`noNext`).** It ends "Pick a tune to begin." and waits for a song; closing it (× or Escape) still works. The test checks that every `noNext` tip has a `doneWhen`, so none can strand the tour.
- **2026-10-07 — The tour order.** Before a song: the welcome. After loading: the staff and Play tip, then "find home" on the key question, then a follow-up on "Help me find it" for people who don't read music. After a key is chosen: number keys, "Hover a chord" (anchored to the staff, since that's where you click), the shapes tip once a chord exists, transpose and re-key, and the tutor last.
- **2026-10-07 — The staff and Play tip comes right after loading, not after the key is chosen.** Listening is how you find home, so "Press Play and watch" is step one; pressing Play finishes it and "find home" appears while the tune plays. Its `when` ends at `keyChosen`, so someone who chooses a key straight away never sees it. "Click any note to try chords" moved out of it to the chord tip, because in a demo the dropdown can't offer chords until the key is chosen. _Rejected:_ after the key is chosen (the person has already played the tune by then).
- **2026-10-07 — Deviation from the brief: no tip sits beside the open chord dropdown.** The brief offered "anchor to the open dropdown, or to #chords once a chord exists"; we took the second. The dropdown closes on any pointer-down outside it, so clicking a tip's Next beside it closed the dropdown under the reader (seen in the browser walk). At 1440x900 no spot beside the open dropdown kept the staff notes, the key question, and the tutor clear. And the dropdown already says "Hover, focus, or tap a chord to hear it under the tune." So "Hover a chord" now shows before the dropdown opens, anchored to the staff, and tells you to click a note (with the chord gloss). Opening the dropdown finishes it. "Blue circle means home" shows beside the chord row (#chords) once a chord exists, where the chips carry the shapes and colors. Every tip after the key is chosen waits while the dropdown is open. _Rejected:_ stopping the tip's pointer events from reaching the dropdown's outside-click handler (works by accident of event order, and still leaves nowhere clear to put the tip).
- **2026-10-07 — "Help me find it" is a `part`: a selector inside the anchor (`[aria-controls='key-finder']` inside #key-prompt).** The button has no id, and other streams' files are off limits. The anchor (#key-prompt) still counts as the tip's subject for the fold rule below. _Rejected:_ asking D3 for an id (a cross-stream change for one selector).
- **2026-10-07 — A tip shows only while its subject is in view (`isOnScreen`: any of it above the keyboard dock; the dock itself is always in view).** The page re-checks on scroll as well as on DOM changes, coalesced to one look-up per frame. After choosing a chord at 1440x900 the chord row is below the fold, and the shapes tip pointed at nothing. Now it waits, the next tip whose subject is in view shows, and the shapes tip appears when the chords scroll into view. The in-view rule is only for tips that appear on their own; Next is covered below. _Rejected:_ scrolling to a tip that appears on its own (moves the page under someone who just acted).
- **2026-10-07 — Next goes to the next tip whose subject is on the page, in view or not, and scrolls it into view.** Review found that with Next limited to tips in view, the shapes tip was unreachable: after a chord is placed, #chords sits below the dock at 1440x900 and 1280x800, so Next skipped it, the tutor tip read "Got it", and the shapes tip later popped up unprompted on scroll. Now Next picks the next due tip whose anchor exists, holds it as current while the existing `scrollForTip` scroll brings its anchor into view, then shows it. "Next" versus "Got it" is computed the same way. _Rejected:_ keeping Next to tips in view (strands any tip below the fold).
- **2026-10-07 — The fold rule: one action elsewhere is free; the second folds the tip into a small "Tip" chip beside the toggle.** An action is a pointer-down, or a key press that activates or changes the focused control (Enter, Space, or an arrow; `isActivatingKey`). Moving focus is not an action: counting it folded every tip for a keyboard-only user before they could Tab to its subject (review finding). The fold is announced in the tip's live region: "Tip folded; press Tip to show it again." Actions on the tip, its anchor element, or the tips toggle don't count. The chip (named "Show the tip: title") brings it back with focus in it. A new tip (its subject appeared, or the folded one was finished by an action) shows open. Folded tips are remembered for the visit only. This is the simplest rule that never leaves a tip over someone working elsewhere, and the chip in the masthead covers nothing. The count is pure (`countAction`) and tested. _Rejected:_ hiding the tip until the user comes back to its subject (needs a notion of "coming back" per tip, and a tip hidden that way is hard to find again); folding on the first action elsewhere (a stray click to unlock sound would fold the welcome).
- **2026-10-07 — Placement keeps the tutor's heading, question box, and replies clear (`#tutor > h2`, `#tutor .log`, `#tutor .ask`), and the open chord dropdown.** These join the key question's controls, Play, the masthead toggles, and the staff, all in `KEEP_CLEAR`.
- **2026-10-07 — Placement slides past controls.** Each candidate spot that covers a control is also tried moved just past it, right, left, up, or down, and those spots once more, for a spot hemmed in on two sides (under the music and left of the tutor). Over the anchor's own top right joins over its top left. The first spot that covers nothing still wins, in the same preference order. Without this, "find home" at 1440x900 covered the home-note buttons by 15 px, and "Same tune, any key" covered the tutor. _Rejected:_ searching a grid of positions (slower on every scroll, and the result is harder to predict).
- **2026-10-07 — The tip is 22rem wide (was 20rem), capped at the screen width less 16 px.** At 20rem the two-line titles made tips about 210 px tall, more than the gap between the music and the key question's buttons at 1280x800. 24rem no longer fit between "Help me find it" and the tutor column. _Rejected:_ 24rem (covered "Help me find it" at both laptop sizes).
- **2026-10-07 — Copy (Matthew owns it; kept short).** Kept: "These number keys are notes. 1 is home.", the chord gloss, and the shape definitions. Changed: the welcome ends "Pick a tune to begin."; "find home" adds "and the key is named after it", to gloss key; new "Don't read music? Use your ear.": "Press Help me find it. You'll hear the tune over a few chords, one at a time. Pick the one that sounds like home."; "Hover a chord" now begins "Click any note on the staff to open its chords." and drops "Try a few and keep the one that sounds right to you" for length (the dropdown says how to hear and choose); "Same tune, any key" names its control ("Open Change key or transpose, above the music"), since on a crowded screen the tip can't always sit right beside it, and drops the line about the label buttons.
- **2026-10-07 — Verified in a browser at 1440x900 and 1280x800** (a throwaway Playwright script, fixture mode): every tip appeared with its subject and covered none of the key question's controls, Play, the masthead toggles, the staff's notes, the dock, the open dropdown, or the tutor's heading, question box, and reply. Nothing showed while the dropdown was open. Two clicks elsewhere folded the welcome into the chip; the chip brought it back, and loading a song moved on to the staff tip.
- **2026-10-07 — Deviation: the e2e rule for dock tips is "never overlaps the dock and stays in the viewport", not "within 150 px above the dock".** `tests/e2e/tips.spec.js` now walks the tour as a beginner does (welcome, load, Play, find home, choose D, place a chord, Next to the tutor's "Got it"), and placement may slide a dock tip further up to keep the tutor or staff clear, which is what matters to the reader. The spec still checks that no tip covers the key question's buttons and chips, Play, the masthead toggles, the staff's notes, or the tutor's heading, question box, and replies. _Rejected:_ keeping the 150 px bound (couples the test to one placement heuristic, not to the reader's need).
