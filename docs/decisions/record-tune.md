# Record a tune: decision log

Matthew, Oct 7: "a hero feature for this is the ability to record your own noodling and then put chords underneath it. Also of course, you need to title it." Branch `feature/record-tune`. One bullet per decision, with what was rejected.

## Rhythm

- **2026-10-07 — A tapped key reads as its full gap; a rest needs a held note and a full beat of silence (Matthew's prerequisite).** A press under 250 ms, or under half a beat, is a tap and lasts until the next onset. A note held half a beat or longer, then followed by at least a beat of silence, keeps its held length and leaves a rest. Tested by tapping Ode to Joy at 108 BPM with 80–150 ms presses, which recovers its exact rhythm, half and dotted notes included. The old test "turns a pause over half a beat after a release into a rest" pressed for 0.4 beat, which is now a tap, so it became two tests: a tapped note fills its gap, and a held note followed by a full beat of silence leaves a rest. Also logged in `a-theory.md`. _Rejected:_ a rest after any full beat of silence, taps included (a tapped half note becomes a quarter and a rest, so the Ode test fails at every half note); a fixed millisecond tap threshold alone (at 200 BPM a 150 ms tap is half a beat).
- **2026-10-07 — A tapped last note lasts until Stop.** `guessRhythm(events, { endMs })` snaps the time from the last onset to Stop (at most four beats, like any gap). A last note that was held keeps its held length. While the take runs, the staff shows a tapped last note as one beat, so it doesn't grow on screen while the user thinks. _Rejected:_ always one beat (every take ends on a quarter note, and most tunes end long); filling to the end of the bar (a guess about the meter, which is still provisional).
- **2026-10-07 — Defaults: tempo from `beatMs`, 4/4 with no pickup, and a provisional C.** The tempo is rounded and kept in the schema's 30–240. The key is provisional, so the existing key question runs next; a recorded tune asks it at once, however short, instead of waiting for the eight notes free play waits for. _Rejected:_ guessing the key from the take (the user's ear does the finding, as with the demos); hiding labels as a demo does (the user knows their own tune; tentative labels on the provisional C are the free-play behavior).

## Capture

- **2026-10-07 — Capture listens to liveNotes, the one place every input presses notes.** `onNoteEvent(listener)` reports a note starting (its first holder) and ending (its last). Chord-row chords now hold their notes under a `chord:` source, so record mode leaves them out of the melody while they still sound. _Rejected:_ a second keyboard listener in record mode (misses the on-screen piano and double-counts); capturing chords (the song is one melody line; chords go on afterwards).
- **2026-10-07 — Monophonic: the later onset cuts the earlier note.** A key still held at Stop ends at Stop. Two keys pressed within a quarter of a beat stay one note, as `guessRhythm` already merges slips, and the note keeps the later key's pitch. _Rejected:_ keeping the earlier pitch (the later press is the one the user meant).
- **2026-10-07 — Escape stops a take.** It's the key people reach for to end a mode. No note or chord key uses it, and the menus it closes elsewhere (the chord picker, the key finder) aren't open while recording. An Escape another handler has already marked handled (a tip closing) is left alone. Escape also disarms an armed flat (`-`), which does no harm. _Rejected:_ Space (a focused on-screen piano key plays with Space or Enter, which is how keyboard-only users play, so Space can't also mean Stop); a letter off the note rows such as Z or P (no mnemonic, and next to the keys being played); Enter (also plays a focused piano key).
- **2026-10-07 — The take is on the staff as it is played.** Each note reloads the tune with the rhythm guessed so far, batched to one redraw per frame. It was cheap to build: the cost is one `guessRhythm` (quadratic in the notes, about 160k comparisons at 400) and one abcjs redraw per frame at most. The beat can shift early in a take as the guess firms up. _Rejected:_ showing the staff only at Stop (the user can't see the tune forming).
- **2026-10-07 — A take stops at 400 notes, the song store's limit, and says so.** The bar shows "That's 400 notes, the most a tune holds, so recording stopped." The 400 notes fit the tutor snapshot's 400-bar bound. _Rejected:_ dropping notes silently past the limit.

## Undo, discard, re-record

- **2026-10-07 — Record again is one undoable step; Discard has its own Undo.** Recording again over one of the user's tunes replaces its notes and tempo with `replaceTake` in a single commit, so the app's Undo (and Cmd-Z) brings the earlier take back, chords included. Discard takes the tune off the shelf, forgets its saved copy, and goes back to what was open before it was recorded (a demo, another tune, or the welcome). The bar then offers Undo until another song opens. _Rejected:_ discard through song history (history is per song and is cleared when another song opens); a confirmation dialog (an undo is quicker and doesn't interrupt).
- **2026-10-07 — While a take runs, the song picker and Record buttons are disabled.** Another song opened from elsewhere (the guided tour) ends the take. _Rejected:_ letting the picker switch mid-take (the take would land on the wrong song).

## Title

- **2026-10-07 — On Stop, an inline field asks for the title, prefilled "My tune" (then "My tune 2", and so on) and focused with the text selected.** Enter or Save keeps it, clicking away keeps what was typed, and Escape keeps the prefilled title. Blank keeps the prefill, whitespace collapses, and titles are cut to the schema's 120 characters. Clicking the title in the bar reopens the field; `song.rename` is an undoable store action. _Rejected:_ a modal dialog (Matthew asked for inline); prefilling a date (long, and it reads as a file name); editing the staff's abcjs title in place (it's SVG text, not a field, and redraws).
- **2026-10-07 — The title goes to the tutor in the snapshot, as data.** The prompt already escapes it inside `<snapshot>`. Tests send a title that tries to close the tag. _Rejected:_ leaving user titles out of the snapshot (the title is context the tutor can use, and it's already treated as data).

## Picker and storage

- **2026-10-07 — The masthead picker is one select, grouped "Demo tunes" and "Your tunes", with a Record button beside it.** It fits about six demo tunes and any number of the user's own on one line, at every width. The welcome keeps its cards: "Load a song" (the demos) and "Play a melody" ("Record a tune", then the user's tunes). The golden-path spec now picks songs with `selectOption`. _Rejected:_ a row of buttons (it crowds the masthead past three or four tunes); a custom menu button (more code and keyboard handling than a native select, for the same result).
- **2026-10-07 — Recorded tunes live in sessionStorage with the same per-song memory, and `src/store/storage.js` is the one swap point.** Each tune is saved under its id like a demo. A small index (`hearhear.myTunes`) lists the user's tune ids, so the picker can offer them after a reload. `songStorage()` is what both read. Returning `window.localStorage` there moves all song memory (demos too) to localStorage. To move only recordings, route by id: user tunes start with `mine-`. _Rejected:_ localStorage now (Matthew chose sessionStorage for song memory); scanning every storage key for songs (slower, and it can't keep the order the tunes were made).
- **2026-10-07 — The recording red is the dominant's red token, `--fn-dominant`.** It is always shown with a shape (a dot) and the word "Recording", so it never stands for the dominant. A color pass is underway on another branch, so this branch adds no token. _Rejected:_ a new `--record` token now (a merge conflict in `tokens.css` with the color pass; it can alias `--fn-dominant` later).
