# Record a tune: decision log

Matthew, Oct 7: "a hero feature for this is the ability to record your own noodling and then put chords underneath it. Also of course, you need to title it." Branch `feature/record-tune`. One bullet per decision, with what was rejected.

## Rhythm

- **2026-10-07 — A tapped key reads as its full gap; a rest needs a held note and a full beat of silence (Matthew's prerequisite).** A press under 250 ms, or under half a beat, is a tap and lasts until the next onset. A note held half a beat or longer, then followed by at least a beat of silence, keeps its held length and leaves a rest. Tested by tapping Ode to Joy at 108 BPM with 80–150 ms presses, which recovers its exact rhythm, half and dotted notes included. The old test "turns a pause over half a beat after a release into a rest" pressed for 0.4 beat, which is now a tap, so it became two tests: a tapped note fills its gap, and a held note followed by a full beat of silence leaves a rest. Also logged in `a-theory.md`. _Rejected:_ a rest after any full beat of silence, taps included (a tapped half note becomes a quarter and a rest, so the Ode test fails at every half note); a fixed millisecond tap threshold alone (at 200 BPM a 150 ms tap is half a beat).
- **2026-10-07 — Swung eighths are written straight, the jazz convention (Matthew, for playing St. James swung).** A long-short pair that fills one beat, at a ratio from 3:2 to 3:1, becomes two eighths. The accepted band is 5:4 to 3.6:1, because tapped timing wobbles by 10–20 ms and the short note of a hard swing is only 120–150 ms. The beat is the most common gap, unless the most common sum of a swung pair accounts for more of the take's gaps (a line of swung eighths alternates two gaps, and neither is the beat). The slip threshold for two keys pressed together dropped from a quarter of a beat to a fifth, so a 3:1 pair's short note isn't merged as a slip. Tests: a tapped 2:1 eighth line comes back as straight eighths at the right tempo; 3:2, 2:1 and 3:1 pairs among quarters come back straight; straight eighths and a dotted quarter are left alone. _Rejected:_ triplet or dotted-eighth notation for swing (the clutter Matthew described); a fixed 2:1 ratio (real swing varies with tempo).
- **2026-10-07 — The swing flag is computed but not written to the song.** `guessRhythm` and `takeNotes` return `swing: true` when more of the take's eighth pairs were swung than straight. The song schema has no `swing` field on this branch (another stream is adding it), and the schema forbids unknown fields, so writing it would fail validation. Once the field lands, `recordedSong` can copy `take.swing`. _Rejected:_ adding the schema field here (it's the other stream's, and two versions would conflict).
- **2026-10-07 — A recorded tune opens with its key and its meter marked provisional, and goes straight to the key step.** `key.provisional` and `meter.provisional` are both `true`, and both are already required fields of `song.schema.json`, so the workspace stream's Rhythm step can read `meter.provisional` as "the meter and tempo are still a guess" and clear it when the user confirms. _Rejected:_ a separate `tempo` flag (the tempo and meter are guessed together from one take, and the schema already carries the meter's flag).
- **2026-10-07 — The beat is read in a plausible tempo, 50–140 BPM.** Most common gap still finds the beat, then it is doubled while it implies more than 140 BPM (the gap is an eighth) and halved while it implies under 50, a deterministic rule. Without it, St. James, which moves mostly in eighths, recorded at double values: its eighths at quarter = 76 imply 152 BPM. The band is wider than an octave, so one step never jumps across it. Tests: tapping St. James straight at 76 recovers its encoded values and a tempo within 3 BPM of 76; Ode to Joy (108) and the swung lines still pass; 200 BPM taps read as eighths at 100, and 37.5 BPM taps as halves at 75. The coordinator suggested an upper bound of about 160 BPM; that leaves St. James at 152 untouched, so the bound is 140. Trade-off: a tune tapped in quarters faster than 140 reads as eighths at half the tempo, which is easy to fix by ear and rarer than an eighth-note tune at a moderate tempo. _Rejected:_ 160 BPM (misses St. James); guessing from the mix of note lengths (a quarter-and-half tune like Twinkle at 120 looks like an eighth-and-quarter tune at 60).
- **2026-10-07 — A tapped last note lasts until Stop.** `guessRhythm(events, { endMs })` snaps the time from the last onset to Stop (at most four beats, like any gap). A last note that was held keeps its held length. While the take runs, the staff shows a tapped last note as one beat, so it doesn't grow on screen while the user thinks. _Rejected:_ always one beat (every take ends on a quarter note, and most tunes end long); filling to the end of the bar (a guess about the meter, which is still provisional).
- **2026-10-07 — Defaults: tempo from `beatMs`, 4/4 with no pickup, and a provisional C.** The tempo is rounded and kept in the schema's 30–240. The key is provisional, so the existing key question runs next; a recorded tune asks it at once, however short, instead of waiting for the eight notes free play waits for. _Rejected:_ guessing the key from the take (the user's ear does the finding, as with the demos); hiding labels as a demo does (the user knows their own tune; tentative labels on the provisional C are the free-play behavior).

## Capture

- **2026-10-07 — Capture listens to liveNotes, the one place every input presses notes.** `onNoteEvent(listener)` reports a note starting (its first holder) and ending (its last). Chord-row chords now hold their notes under a `chord:` source, so record mode leaves them out of the melody while they still sound. _Rejected:_ a second keyboard listener in record mode (misses the on-screen piano and double-counts); capturing chords (the song is one melody line; chords go on afterwards).
- **2026-10-07 — Monophonic: the later onset cuts the earlier note.** A key still held at Stop ends at Stop. Two keys pressed within a quarter of a beat stay one note, as `guessRhythm` already merges slips, and the note keeps the later key's pitch. _Rejected:_ keeping the earlier pitch (the later press is the one the user meant).
- **2026-10-07 — Escape stops a take.** It's the key people reach for to end a mode. No note or chord key uses it, and the menus it closes elsewhere (the chord picker, the key finder) aren't open while recording. An Escape another handler has already marked handled (a tip closing) is left alone. Escape also disarms an armed flat (`-`), which does no harm. _Rejected:_ Space (a focused on-screen piano key plays with Space or Enter, which is how keyboard-only users play, so Space can't also mean Stop); a letter off the note rows such as Z or P (no mnemonic, and next to the keys being played); Enter (also plays a focused piano key).
- **2026-10-07 — The take is on the staff as it is played.** Each note reloads the tune with the rhythm guessed so far, batched to one redraw per frame. It was cheap to build: the cost is one `guessRhythm` (quadratic in the notes, about 160k comparisons at 400) and one abcjs redraw per frame at most. The beat can shift early in a take as the guess firms up. _Rejected:_ showing the staff only at Stop (the user can't see the tune forming).
- **2026-10-07 — A take stops at 400 notes, the song store's limit, and says so.** The bar shows "That's 400 notes, the most a tune holds, so recording stopped." The 400 notes fit the tutor snapshot's 400-bar bound. _Rejected:_ dropping notes silently past the limit.

## Undo, discard, re-record

- **2026-10-07 — Record again is one undoable step; Discard has its own Undo.** Recording again over one of the user's tunes replaces its notes and tempo with `replaceTake` in a single commit, so the app's Undo (and Cmd-Z) brings the earlier take back, chords included. Discard takes the tune off the shelf, forgets its saved copy, and goes back to what was open before it was recorded (a demo, another tune, or the welcome). The bar then offers Undo until another song opens. _Rejected:_ discard through song history (history is per song and is cleared when another song opens); a confirmation dialog (an undo is quicker and doesn't interrupt).
- **2026-10-07 — While a take runs, the song picker and Record buttons are disabled.** Another song opened from elsewhere (the guided tour) ends the take. _Rejected:_ letting the picker switch mid-take (the take would land on the wrong song).

## Title

- **2026-10-07 — On Stop, an inline field asks for the title, prefilled "My tune 1" (then "My tune 2", and so on; a title is required) and focused with the text selected.** Enter or Save keeps it, clicking away keeps what was typed, and Escape keeps the prefilled title. Blank keeps the prefill, whitespace collapses, and titles are cut to the schema's 120 characters. Clicking the title in the bar reopens the field; `song.rename` is an undoable store action. _Rejected:_ a modal dialog (Matthew asked for inline); prefilling a date (long, and it reads as a file name); editing the staff's abcjs title in place (it's SVG text, not a field, and redraws).
- **2026-10-07 — The title goes to the tutor in the snapshot, as data.** The prompt already escapes it inside `<snapshot>`. Tests send a title that tries to close the tag. _Rejected:_ leaving user titles out of the snapshot (the title is context the tutor can use, and it's already treated as data).

## Picker and storage

- **2026-10-07 — The masthead picker is one select, grouped "Demo tunes" and "Your tunes", with a Record button beside it.** It fits about six demo tunes and any number of the user's own on one line, at every width. The welcome keeps its cards: "Load a song" (the demos) and "Play a melody" ("Record a tune", then the user's tunes). The golden-path spec now picks songs with `selectOption`. _Rejected:_ a row of buttons (it crowds the masthead past three or four tunes); a custom menu button (more code and keyboard handling than a native select, for the same result).
- **2026-10-07 — Recorded tunes live in sessionStorage with the same per-song memory, and `src/store/storage.js` is the one swap point.** Each tune is saved under its id like a demo. persist.js keeps one "my songs" index (`hearhear.mySongs`, read with `mySongs()`) of the user's song ids, and the picker lists user songs from it alone. `songStorage()` is what both read. Returning `window.localStorage` there moves all song memory (demos too) to localStorage. To move only recordings, route by id: user tunes start with `mine-`. _Rejected:_ localStorage now (Matthew chose sessionStorage for song memory); scanning every storage key for songs (slower, and it can't keep the order the tunes were made).
- **2026-10-07 — The recording red is the dominant's red token, `--fn-dominant`.** It is always shown with a shape (a dot) and the word "Recording", so it never stands for the dominant. A color pass is underway on another branch, so this branch adds no token. _Rejected:_ a new `--record` token now (a merge conflict in `tokens.css` with the color pass; it can alias `--fn-dominant` later).

## Gate review fixes (PR 40)

- **2026-10-07 — A dotted figure is never read as swing.** A dotted quarter and eighth is a 3:1 pair over two beats. When such pairs filled most of a take, their sum won as a "swing beat", and the take came out as even eighths at 50 BPM with `swing: true`. Now a long-short pair whose sum is twice a gap the take also plays is a dotted figure. The swing reading also needs its beat under 1.5 times the straight beat. When dotted figures are found, half their sum becomes the beat if more of the take lands on the grid that way. Tested with three dotted pairs and two quarters, which come back dotted at the right tempo. _Rejected:_ the 1.5× bound alone (the most common gap in that take is the dotted quarter itself, so the swing beat passed the bound).
- **2026-10-07 — A re-take is a draft until Stop.** Record again used to show the take under the tune's own id, so per-song memory saved it over the tune while it ran. If another song opened mid-take (the tour's "Try it", or a reload), the old take and its chords were gone with no undo. Now the take is shown under a draft id (the tune's id plus `-take`). The saved tune and the shelf's copy are untouched until Stop writes the new take in one undoable step. A dropped take only forgets its draft. Tested with persistence installed: a re-take cut short by a demo opening leaves the saved notes and chord intact, and a finished re-take saves and forgets its draft. A reload mid-re-take reopens the draft, not on the shelf, while the tune stays in the picker. _Rejected:_ pausing persistence during a take (persist.js is shared, and a pause that a crash skips would lose the take); restoring the tune after the fact (the save order between subscribers isn't something to rely on).
- **2026-10-07 — One Escape does one thing.** The record bar listens for Escape in the capture phase and marks it handled, so it stops a take even with a tip showing, and the tip stays open. With a flat armed (`-`), Escape only disarms the flat: the number row now marks that Escape handled, and the record bar skips it while a flat is armed. _Rejected:_ relying on listener order between components.
- **2026-10-07 — Focus never drops to the page.** Tabbing out of the title field saves the title and puts focus on the title button, as Enter does. Cancelling an armed take (its button or Escape) puts focus back on the Record control that armed it.
- **2026-10-07 — Discard after Undo goes back where the first Discard did.** Undo restores what Discard needs (the song open before the tune, and that the tune was the newest).
- **2026-10-07 — Titles are bounded by code point everywhere.** `isSong` in persist.js counted UTF-16 units while `cleanTitle` and `song.rename` count code points, so a 120-character title with emoji was refused on reload. It now uses `Array.from(title).length`.
- **Known limit, not fixed: a melody note on a pitch the chord row is already holding isn't captured.** liveNotes reports a note starting only when its first holder presses it, so a melody key on a pitch a held chord already sounds raises no event. The take misses that note. It's rare (a melody note doubling a chord tone while the chord is held), and fixing it means per-source events in liveNotes.
- **2026-10-07 — A swung take is marked to play back swung (amends "The swing flag is computed but not written to the song").** The swing stream merged the optional `swing` ratio into the schema, so a new tune whose eighth pairs were mostly swung gets `swing: 2` (triplet swing). Its staff stays in straight eighths with the Swing marking. A straight take has no `swing` field. A re-take keeps the tune's existing `swing`, since `replaceTake` changes only notes and tempo. The swing spec now picks St. James from the picker's select. _Rejected:_ recording the measured ratio (tapped timing is too rough to be worth more than "swung or not").
